Additionally, to address your previous question on datasets and machine learning models more specifically:

Datasets:
- OPSSAT-AD (ESA) - the primary benchmark, containing labeled anomalies across 9 satellite internal telemetry channels from ESA's satellites.
- NOAA SWPC indices - space weather data, timestamped and freely accessible
- ESA Anomaly Dataset - multi-mission labeled dataset for generalization testing

Machine Learning Models:
- Baselines: Isolation Forest and Matrix Profile (STUMPY).
- Neural: LSTM Autoencoder for temporal pattern modeling
- Calibration: Online conformal prediction on reconstruction error.

The core experiment and purpose of this phase is a proof-of-concept & ablation study - running each model with telemetry-only vs. telemetry + space weather features - to demonstrate whether external context meaningfully improves anomaly detection.
